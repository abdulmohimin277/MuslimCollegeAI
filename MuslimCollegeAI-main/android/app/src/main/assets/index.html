Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Muslim College AI Study Agent collects and how it is used.

  • What we store: Your name, roll number, and the Gemini API key you enter are saved only in your own browser using local storage. Your conversation history also stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any Muslim College server.
  • No servers, no tracking: This app runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate accounts, track your activity, or use analytics cookies.
  • Google Gemini API: When you ask a question, the question is sent directly to the Google Generative Language API using the API key you provided. Google's privacy policy applies to that request: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Voice input: Voice typing uses your browser's built-in Web Speech API and is processed locally by the browser; it is not stored by this app.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • Deleting your data: Use the "Clear Data" button in the Tools panel, or clear your browser site data / local storage at any time.
