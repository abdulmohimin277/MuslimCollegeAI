Muslim Group of Schools & Colleges

Multan · Pakistan

Muslim Group of Schools & Colleges was established on 1 September 1994. Through quality education it has proven its significance and paved the way for progress and prosperity — not only for its students but also for the country. Its highly qualified, devoted and dedicated teaching faculty has striven to decorate the highway of its history with garlands of positions in the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Multan.

On 10 October 2000, the institution was affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Multan. In 2004 it was granted the status of a Higher Secondary School and, in 2010, promoted to a College. On the strong demand of parents and students, this institution has earned a fine reputation among the government and private schools and colleges of Multan.
